# Model weights

The learned weights in `model.js`, including the model fine-tuned on the
Roshambo.me research export, are distributed under
[CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/).
This notice covers learned weights. The project's source code retains its
existing license.

The Roshambo.me data was provided by Lasse Hassing and published by
Elias Bouacida and Renaud Foucart in *Rituals of Reasons: Experimental Evidence
on the Social Acceptability of Lotteries in Allocation Problems* (2024).
The data and its license are available at
[doi:10.57745/NZTMN9](https://entrepot.recherche.data.gouv.fr/dataset.xhtml?persistentId=doi:10.57745/NZTMN9)
and in the [publisher's license file](https://entrepot.recherche.data.gouv.fr/api/access/datafile/398329).

The model also uses the earlier research datasets attributed in
[README.md](README.md). Raw participant logs are not included in this repository.
